I am a writer and advocate based in Perth. As Advocacy & Policy Director at Domestic Violence Advocacy Australia (DVAA), I lead submissions to government consultations and design the community surveys that inform them. My work brings the knowledge of people with lived experience into law reform. I also write opinion pieces and creative nonfiction on justice and the systems that shape people’s lives.

The work below is complete and unedited for this site, apart from the removal of identifying details. Each piece was written for a real decision-maker and a real outcome.
