It includes a safety check and an age check before any questions, an exit page with support services, plain-language explanations of terms used in each state, and online safety information at the end.

The survey questions

A domestic violence order is a court order that sets out rules to protect someone from a partner, former partner, or family member. Throughout Australia, this type of order is known by different names:

  • New South Wales: Apprehended Domestic Violence Order (ADVO)
  • Queensland and the Northern Territory: Domestic Violence Order
  • Victoria: Family Violence Intervention Order
  • Western Australia: Family Violence Restraining Order
  • South Australia: Intervention Order
  • Tasmania and the ACT: Family Violence Order

In this survey, the term “domestic violence order” refers to any one of these. Breaking one of the rules in a domestic violence order is called a breach, and it is a crime. Unless a question is marked Required, you may leave it blank. Follow-up questions are optional and apply only where their wording applies to your experience.

Thank you for your time and sharing your experience. If completing the survey has brought up difficult feelings or concerns, support is available.

  • 1800RESPECT: 1800 737 732. Support for domestic, family and sexual violence.
  • Lifeline: 13 11 14. Crisis support and suicide prevention counselling.
  • If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000.

Online safety: If you are using a shared device or account, other people may be able to see your browser history, downloads, open tabs, saved passwords, form activity or notifications. If it is safe for you to do so, consider using a private browser window and clearing your browser history after completing the survey. Be aware that clearing browser history can sometimes be noticeable, can remove information you may need, and may not remove all records from a shared device, account or network. Follow your own safety plan and only take these steps if they are safe for you.

Please take care of yourself and seek support when you are able to do so safely.
